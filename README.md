## What I'm working on

- **Rust everywhere** — Actix and Axum on the server, Iced and Tauri on the desktop, and a handful of crates published to [crates.io](https://crates.io/users/Drew-Chase).
- **Internal tooling at Mardens** — purchase-order tracking, label and barcode printing, pricing systems. Rust + React/TypeScript, containerized, running on AWS.
- **Self-hosted infrastructure** — TrueNAS SCALE, Docker, and a pile of services I maintain because I'd rather own the box than rent the abstraction.
